# Supplied EPUBs: source ledger and supplemental form plates

Seven local books were consulted progressively for course enrichment. **The club curriculum remains authoritative for app IDs, grading requirements and terminology.** Book guidance is supplemental, tradition-specific evidence, not a replacement syllabus or medical advice. Only eight visually identified full-form Woo plates are extracted; no EPUB is published or linked by the application.

## Reading method and scope

Read ZIP members with Python `zipfile` and XML parsing; no DRM bypass. Resolve `META-INF/container.xml` → `content.opf` → manifest/spine, then inspect chapter text, captions and repeated image references. Locators below are internal archive members, not web URLs or assumed printed page numbers. OPF dates describe the supplied metadata, not independently verified edition histories. No whole-book transcription or translation is integrated here.

Progress: bibliographic metadata and spine/front matter inspected for all seven; relevant chapter/heading text sampled progressively; Woo overview and all eight chapter-to-image associations checked, and all eight selected plates opened visually at original resolution. Gold's text is concentrated in one large member and many tables are images; plain-text extraction alone is incomplete. Other books' images have not been individually identity-verified and are not extracted.
