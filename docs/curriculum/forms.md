## Unverified

- Hangul for all Hyong is copied verbatim from Formen §4.3.3 (pp.12-17), not checked against a Korean source; Gwang-Gae is 광개 (ITF usually 광개토).
- Chung-Ang is spelled Chung-Jang in the 6. Dan row of the exam program; the Formen PDF headline says CHUNG-ANG but gives hangul 충장 (= Chung-Jang).
- Club spellings differ from ITF (Chung-Gun/Joong-Gun, Chung-Mu/Choong-Moo).
- Historical dates are copied as printed (Ge-Baek 600 AD, Won-Hyo 868 AD) and may be errors in the PDF.
- Spanish and English texts are neutral translations of the German PDF.
- Poomse for Dan 2-6 (Koryo ... Ilyo) are listed here only, not in the JSON.
- Page numbers in the JSON are printed page numbers of the Formen PDF (PDF page = printed + 4).
