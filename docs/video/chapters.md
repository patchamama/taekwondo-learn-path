## What is NOT in the course (as far as I could verify)
- A plain **twio-ap-chagi** (jumping front kick): only a jump *side* kick and scissor front kicks are shown.
- **an-naeryo-chagi / packat-naeryo-chagi**: the course shows *an-chagi / bakkat-chagi* (crescent kicks), which are related but not the same name.
- **hansonnal-arae-macki** (low knife-hand block) and the **fist-reinforced block** were not identified.
- Rolls / falls (nak-bop) and the club's saju drills.
